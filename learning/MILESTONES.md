# Quantum Purity Noise Budget: 30 milestones

This learner-independent map follows the selected educational anchor, John Watrous, *The Theory of Quantum Information* (Cambridge University Press, 2018). The first 14 milestones build background and preliminaries from local objects to the full discrimination framework. M15–M30 develop the research narrative from the moment identity to the exact logarithmic threshold and its limits. These are understanding targets, not quizzes or personal learning records. See [Watrous bridge](WATROUS_BRIDGE.md).
